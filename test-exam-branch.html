About Exam Branch

The Examination Branch is headed by the Controller of Examinations, Dr. M.A. RABBANI (B.E., M.E., Ph.D.), and the Principal is the Chief Controller of Examinations. The office of the Controller of Examinations was established in the year 2016 after the conferment of Autonomous status to the institute.

The Examination Branch is responsible for conducting all examinations, maintaining academic records, and ensuring the smooth functioning of the examination process.

Responsibilities

The examination branch handles various responsibilities including:

  • Conducting semester examinations
  • Maintaining student records
  • Issuing certificates and transcripts
  • Managing examination schedules
